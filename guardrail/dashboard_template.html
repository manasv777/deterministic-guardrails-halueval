Honest limitations

Small sample

One subset

HaluEval QA only. Dialogue, summarization and general-chat hallucinations were not tested.

Heuristic key facts

Key facts are found with regex: numbers, dates, capitalized spans. Spelled-out numbers and lowercase facts are missed; some sentence-initial words are caught by mistake.

Not CTGT's method

This is an independent experiment testing a similar idea. It is not a reproduction of CTGT's system or results.
